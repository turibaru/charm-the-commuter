# Charm the Commuter: Forecast a City That Isn't

A one-line weather forecast for Quillhaven, a city that doesn't exist, where a light drizzle of confetti falls from a parade that never ends — highs of 19°, so commuters should bring an umbrella and a dance partner. Written to charm a bored commuter on the way to work, in the rhythm of a real broadcast forecast, bent just enough to make them smile.
